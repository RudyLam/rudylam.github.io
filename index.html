About

Rudy Lam

MSc Thesis Student @ SFU Computing Science, Burnaby

I am a thesis-based MSc student in Computing Science at Simon Fraser University, advised by Prof. Andrea Tagliasacchi. Before starting graduate school, I worked as a Software Engineer at Ciena. I hold a Bachelor of Applied Science in Engineering Physics from the University of British Columbia.

My research sits at the intersection of computer vision and computer graphics, with a focus on reconstructing 3D surfaces from 2D images. In particular, I am interested in structured-light reconstruction driven by photometric cues, without the specialized hardware it traditionally requires.
